For Those Who Need to Wrestle

What if you're not broken—just surrounded by people who've stopped asking questions?

There's a space where doubt isn't a defect. Where you can say "I don't know" and belong anyway. Let's walk together.

Watch the invitation

I used to think faith was something you either had or you didn't—like being good at math or having brown eyes.

I'd watch people around me seem so certain, so peaceful, and I'd wonder why I couldn't just... believe.

"If you've ever felt like you're missing some spiritual gene everyone else got, you're not alone."

I tried the church thing. I really did.

But every time I asked a hard question, I got that look. You know the one. Like I was broken for wondering. Like doubt was something to fix instead of something to feel.

I wanted real conversation. I got small talk and building fund appeals. I wanted to understand what I believed. I got told to just have faith.

Here's what nobody told me: You're not broken. You're not less-than because you need to wrestle with this stuff.

The problem isn't you. It's that most spaces are built for people who already believe—not for people who are still figuring it out.

Someone invited me into this community. Just like I'm inviting you now.
