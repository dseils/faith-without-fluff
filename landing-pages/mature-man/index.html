The Real Issue

I didn't walk away from faith because I stopped believing. I walked away because I couldn't pretend anymore.

Couldn't pretend the questions didn't matter. Couldn't pretend the answers satisfied. Couldn't pretend that showing up on Sunday was the same as actually believing.

I wanted substance. I got politics. I wanted honesty. I got performance.

"Doubt isn't the enemy of faith. Dishonesty is."

The problem isn't that we have questions. It's that we've been taught to bury them. To perform certainty we don't feel. To act like we've arrived when we're still very much on the journey.

You're not broken for needing something real.
