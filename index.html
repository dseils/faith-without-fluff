Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a church?

No. We're a podcast and online community. There's no denomination, no building, no pastor. Just people exploring faith together.

Do I have to be a Christian to join?

Absolutely not. We have members who are agnostic, atheist, Buddhist, "spiritual but not religious," and everything in between. Curiosity is the only requirement.

What if I have really hard questions?

Bring them. Questions about suffering? The Bible's contradictions? Science and faith? Hell? We've tackled them all. No topic is off-limits.

Will you try to convert me?

No. We're not recruiters. We're explorers. Our goal is to help you find your own path, not to get you to sign on a dotted line.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no exit interviews, no guilt. Cancel from your account dashboard or email us — we'll handle it immediately.
