# Faith Without the Fluff — Brand Identity Guidelines

## The Refined Cairn

### Concept
A cairn (stack of stones) marking a point on a journey. Hand-placed, not machined. Temporary, not monumental. The builders moved on, leaving markers for others.

### Visual Characteristics
- **Hand-placed**: Slightly irregular, balanced but imperfect
- **Grounded**: Largest stone at bottom, anchored
- **Inviting**: Room for one more stone
- **Human-scaled**: Not monumental, approachable
