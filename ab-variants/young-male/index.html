Meet Donald

Donald Seils is a retired ordained minister who's spent decades watching good people get turned away from Christianity—not by Jesus, but by the institutions built around him.

That's why he created Faith Without the Fluff: a space for honest conversation about faith, doubt, and what it really means to follow Jesus without the church politics.

Is This For You?

We're explorers, not enforcers. Here's who this is (and isn't) for:

This IS for you if...

  • You're spiritually curious but church-shy
  • You have questions that feel "forbidden" in Christian spaces
  • You've been hurt by church but still feel drawn to Jesus
  • You value honesty over religious correctness
  • You want to figure out what YOU believe

This is NOT for you if...

  • You want someone to tell you what to believe
  • You're looking for dogma and doctrine
  • You want traditional church services
  • You're not open to questions and exploration
